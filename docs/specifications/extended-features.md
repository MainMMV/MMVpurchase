# MMV Purchases — 60 Additional Features

## 7. Advanced Shopping and Product Management

101. **Shopping List Mode** — Create a checklist for a shopping trip and mark items as purchased, skipped, or unavailable.

102. **Reusable Shopping Lists** — Save templates for groceries, household supplies, office equipment, travel, and other recurring shopping needs.

103. **Shopping Trip Budget** — Set a separate budget for each shopping trip and compare the expected cost against the actual total.

104. **Shopping Basket Calculator** — Add multiple items to a temporary basket and calculate the total before confirming purchases.

105. **Product Variants** — Record product size, color, model, capacity, dimensions, or other specifications.

106. **Product Comparison Workspace** — Compare multiple saved products side by side using price, specifications, quantity, warranty, and notes.

107. **Alternative Product Links** — Attach alternative products to a saved item so you can compare options before purchasing.

108. **Favorite Products** — Mark frequently purchased products as favorites for quick access.

109. **Product Templates** — Save frequently used product details and create new purchase records from them.

110. **Custom Product Fields** — Add user-defined fields such as model number, size, material, expiration period, or compatibility.

111. **Product Photo Gallery** — Attach multiple images to one product, including reference photos, packaging, and condition pictures.

112. **Barcode Scanning** — Scan a product barcode using the device camera to store its barcode number and retrieve a matching product from your own saved records. No external product database is required.

## 8. Household Inventory and Product Maintenance

113. **Home Inventory** — Maintain a list of products and belongings you already own to avoid unnecessarily buying duplicates.

114. **Inventory Locations** — Assign items to locations such as Bedroom, Kitchen, Office, Storage, or Car.

115. **Stock Quantity Tracking** — Track how many units of a household product remain available.

116. **Minimum Stock Thresholds** — Set a minimum quantity for an item and show a restock warning when the available quantity drops below it.

117. **Consumable Refill Tracking** — Record when frequently used supplies such as detergent, toiletries, or printer paper need replenishing.

118. **Expiration Date Tracking** — Store expiry dates for food, medicine, cosmetics, and other dated products, with reminders before expiration.

119. **Product Condition Tracking** — Record whether an owned item is New, Good, Used, Damaged, or Needs Repair.

120. **Maintenance Schedule** — Set maintenance dates for appliances, vehicles, electronics, and other belongings.

121. **Repair History** — Record repair dates, service providers, repair costs, and descriptions for owned products.

122. **Serial Number Registry** — Store serial numbers, model numbers, and device identifiers for electronics and other valuable items.

## 9. Returns, Refunds, and Ownership Records

123. **Return Tracker** — Register an item that may be returned and track its return status.

124. **Return Deadline Reminder** — Save the last eligible return date and receive a reminder before the deadline.

125. **Refund Tracking** — Record expected refunds, received amounts, dates, and outstanding balances.

126. **Exchange Records** — Document product exchanges, including the original item, replacement item, and any additional payment.

127. **Warranty Register** — Store warranty start dates, expiration dates, coverage details, and warranty document references.

128. **Warranty Expiration Alerts** — Send reminders before a saved warranty period ends.

129. **Purchase Ownership Record** — Record who an item was purchased for, such as Personal, Family, Work, or Gift.

130. **Gift Purchase Tracker** — Organize gifts by recipient, occasion, budget, purchase date, and delivery status.

131. **Borrowed and Lent Items** — Track items lent to others or borrowed from someone, including expected return dates.

132. **Purchase Dispute Notes** — Save a timeline of issues with a vendor, relevant dates, reference numbers, and resolution status.

## 10. Advanced Money and Payment Tracking

133. **Split Purchase Transactions** — Divide one purchase across categories, such as a supermarket receipt containing groceries, cleaning products, and personal items.

134. **Partial Payment Tracking** — Record purchases paid in several installments and display the amount paid and outstanding balance.

135. **Installment Plan Calculator** — Enter the purchase amount, down payment, number of installments, interest rate, and fees to calculate the expected payment schedule.

136. **Buy Now, Pay Later Register** — Track deferred-payment purchases, their due dates, remaining balances, and payment status.

137. **Deposit and Reservation Tracking** — Record deposits paid to reserve an item or service, including the remaining amount due.

138. **Cash Wallet Ledger** — Maintain a manual balance for cash on hand and record additions, withdrawals, and purchase deductions.

139. **Account-Based Transactions** — Separate recorded transactions by user-defined accounts or wallets, such as Cash, Main Card, Savings, or Work Funds.

140. **Transfer Between Accounts** — Record money moved between tracked accounts without incorrectly counting the transfer as income or spending.

141. **Purchase Discounts** — Record original price, discount amount, and final price to see how much was saved.

142. **Tax Breakdown** — Store tax amounts separately from the product price and calculate the full purchase total.

143. **Shared-Cost Calculator** — Divide a manually entered expense between multiple people and record how much each person owes. This does not require a multi-user account system.

144. **Expense Reimbursement Tracker** — Track purchases made on behalf of an employer or another person, including the amount expected to be reimbursed and the amount received.

## 11. Advanced Budgets and Saving Tools

145. **Category-Specific Budgets** — Set separate monthly limits for categories such as groceries, electronics, clothing, and entertainment.

146. **Budget Rollover** — Carry an unused portion of one month's category budget into the next month, according to the user's settings.

147. **Envelope Budgeting** — Divide available money into named envelopes such as Essentials, Shopping, Bills, Gifts, and Leisure.

148. **Purchase Reservation** — Reserve part of a budget for a planned purchase so the same money is not accidentally allocated to another goal.

149. **Savings Withdrawal Reasons** — Require or optionally record a reason when withdrawing money from a savings fund.

150. **Savings Contribution Split** — Distribute one recorded contribution across several savings goals using manually selected amounts or percentages.

151. **No-Spend Day Tracker** — Mark days when no purchases were made and view a calendar of completed no-spend days.

152. **Spending Limits by Frequency** — Set limits such as a maximum number of takeaway purchases per week or clothing purchases per month.

153. **Purchase Cooldown Timer** — Set an optional waiting period before a planned nonessential purchase can be marked Ready to Buy.

154. **Savings Challenge Templates** — Create fixed challenges such as saving 10,000 UZS daily, saving a chosen amount each week, or gradually increasing contributions.

155. **Budget Lock** — Lock a completed budget period to prevent accidental changes to its original plan, while allowing corrections through an explicit edit process.

## 12. Productivity, Interface, and Convenience

156. **Home Screen Quick Actions** — Provide shortcuts for adding a purchase, recording a payment, depositing savings, or opening the calendar.

157. **Custom Dashboard Layout** — Allow users to rearrange dashboard widgets and hide sections they do not use.

158. **Compact and Detailed Views** — Switch between a condensed list for quick browsing and a detailed view showing more purchase information.

159. **Custom Navigation** — Let users choose which sections appear in the main navigation and in what order.

160. **Quick Amount Entry** — Provide number-pad shortcuts for common amounts and configurable amount presets.

161. **Snooze Reminders** — Delay a notification by a selected interval without changing the original deadline.

162. **Notification Quiet Hours** — Set periods when noncritical reminders should not make a sound or appear as notifications.

163. **Actionable Notifications** — Allow supported notifications to offer actions such as Mark Paid, Snooze, or Open Purchase.

164. **Recently Viewed Items** — Display recently opened purchases, subscriptions, goals, and records for faster navigation.

165. **Pinned Records** — Pin important purchases, subscriptions, or savings goals to the top of their respective lists.

166. **Custom Date Presets** — Filter records using shortcuts such as This Week, Last Month, Last 90 Days, or Custom Period.

167. **Change History** — Show previous values for selected fields, such as a purchase price or subscription amount, when those edits have been recorded.

168. **Trash and Recovery** — Move deleted records into a temporary trash area and restore them before permanent deletion.

169. **App Usage Preferences** — Configure default purchase categories, default priority, preferred list view, and common form values.

170. **Accessibility Settings** — Support adjustable text sizes, clear contrast, screen-reader labels, and reduced-motion preferences.

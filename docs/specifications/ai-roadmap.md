# MMV Purchases — AI Feature List

## 1. MMV Purchases — Smart Purchase Management

1. **AI Purchase Recognition** — Automatically identify product names, prices, quantities, vendors, and purchase dates from receipts, screenshots, invoices, and manually entered text.

2. **Smart Category Detection** — Automatically categorize purchases into groceries, electronics, clothing, household items, transport, health, entertainment, and custom categories.

3. **Natural Language Purchase Entry** — Add purchases using everyday language, such as “Buy headphones for 800,000 UZS next Friday,” with AI extracting the item, price, and date.

4. **AI Price Comparison** — Compare saved prices for the same product across different vendors and highlight the cheapest available option when comparison data is provided.

5. **Purchase History Intelligence** — Analyze past purchases to identify frequently purchased products, spending patterns, preferred vendors, and changes in purchasing behavior.

6. **Duplicate Purchase Detection** — Identify potentially duplicate entries using product names, prices, dates, quantities, and receipt information.

7. **Smart Purchase Recommendations** — Recommend when to buy, delay, replace, or remove an item based on its priority, budget, previous spending, and upcoming financial commitments.

8. **AI Priority Suggestions** — Suggest Low, Medium, or High priority based on urgency, necessity, expected usage, and the user's stated goals.

9. **Purchase Description Generator** — Generate clear notes for purchases, including intended use, product specifications, warranty details, and reasons for buying.

10. **Spending Pattern Analysis** — Explain where purchase money goes, which categories are increasing, and which buying habits may be unnecessarily expensive.

11. **Smart Vendor Insights** — Identify frequently used vendors, compare historical prices, and show which vendors have provided better prices for comparable items.

12. **AI Purchase Search** — Search using natural-language questions such as “Show electronics bought last month under 2 million UZS.”

13. **Purchase Need Assessment** — Help evaluate whether an item is necessary, optional, a replacement, or an impulse purchase before committing money.

14. **AI Budget Warnings** — Predict when planned purchases may exceed the available budget and suggest lower-cost alternatives, delays, or adjustments.

15. **Receipt and Warranty Intelligence** — Extract purchase dates, totals, vendor details, warranty periods, and return deadlines from uploaded receipts when the information is readable.

## 2. Subscriptions — AI Subscription Management

16. **Automatic Subscription Detection** — Identify potential recurring payments from transaction records or manually entered payment history and suggest creating a subscription record.

17. **Recurring Payment Prediction** — Estimate the next billing date and amount from the subscription schedule and previous payment history.

18. **Subscription Renewal Alerts** — Provide advance reminders before subscription renewals, separating upcoming payments from payments that have already been processed.

19. **Subscription Price Change Detection** — Flag increases or decreases in recorded subscription costs and explain how they affect monthly and yearly spending.

20. **Unused Subscription Identification** — Highlight subscriptions that may be underused when usage information is available, or ask the user to confirm whether the service is still needed.

21. **Subscription Cost Forecasting** — Calculate expected monthly and yearly subscription expenses using recurring amounts, billing intervals, and known future changes.

22. **Duplicate Service Detection** — Identify potentially overlapping services, such as multiple streaming or cloud-storage subscriptions, and let the user decide whether they are necessary.

23. **AI Subscription Savings Plan** — Suggest possible savings by reviewing subscription costs, user-reported usage, duplicate services, and available lower-cost plans.

24. **Free-Trial Expiration Tracking** — Track trial end dates and warn the user before a trial is expected to convert into a paid subscription.

25. **Subscription Cancellation Assistant** — Provide cancellation instructions, official service links when available, and a checklist of steps. The AI must not claim a subscription is cancelled unless cancellation is confirmed.

26. **Annual vs. Monthly Plan Comparison** — Compare the total cost of monthly and annual plans, including the break-even point and the amount saved by choosing annual billing.

27. **Subscription Payment Calendar** — Organize expected renewals chronologically and highlight days when several subscriptions may charge at once.

28. **Subscription Budget Optimization** — Estimate the effect of pausing, downgrading, or cancelling selected subscriptions on future spending.

## 3. Savings — AI Savings Intelligence

29. **Personalized Savings Plan** — Create a savings plan based on the user's target amount, deadline, current savings, income, and expected expenses.

30. **Automatic Savings Suggestions** — Recommend realistic amounts to save weekly or monthly based on the user's budget and upcoming obligations.

31. **Savings Goal Forecasting** — Estimate when the user will reach a savings target at the current saving rate and show how different contribution amounts change the completion date.

32. **AI Savings Breakdown** — Divide a large savings target into smaller milestones, such as weekly deposits, monthly contributions, or percentage-based progress.

33. **Spending-to-Savings Analysis** — Identify spending categories where reductions could free up money for savings without automatically changing the user's budget.

34. **Smart Saving Opportunities** — Suggest potential savings based on recurring expenses, purchase history, unused subscriptions, and discretionary spending patterns.

35. **Emergency Fund Planner** — Help set an emergency-fund target based on user-provided essential monthly expenses and track progress toward that target.

36. **Savings Consistency Analysis** — Detect whether deposits are regular, identify missed contributions, and suggest achievable adjustments to help maintain progress.

37. **Savings Scenario Simulator** — Show how different contribution amounts, spending reductions, or target dates affect the time needed to reach a savings goal.

38. **Savings Milestone Predictions** — Estimate dates for reaching 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of a target, recalculating when balances or contributions change.

39. **Inflation-Aware Savings Planning** — When a suitable inflation assumption is provided or current data is available, estimate how rising prices may affect the future cost of a savings goal.

40. **Savings Protection Alerts** — Warn when planned purchases could reduce savings below a user-defined minimum or interfere with a financial goal.

## 4. Goal-Based Purchases — One-Time Purchases

41. **AI Purchase Goal Creation** — Convert a goal such as “Buy a laptop for 12 million UZS by March” into a structured record with a target price, deadline, priority, and progress.

42. **Purchase Goal Breakdown** — Divide the target amount into manageable savings contributions and show the amount required per week or month to meet the deadline.

43. **Affordability Assessment** — Estimate whether a planned purchase is affordable based on current available funds, expected expenses, subscriptions, and existing savings commitments.

44. **Buy Now vs. Later Analysis** — Compare purchasing immediately with waiting and saving more, using the user's cash availability, deadline, and upcoming commitments.

45. **AI Purchase Readiness Score** — Show a transparent readiness assessment based on saved funds, remaining cost, deadline, and priority rather than an unexplained recommendation.

46. **Goal Deadline Feasibility** — Determine whether a target can realistically be reached by its deadline and suggest a revised contribution amount, lower budget, or later date when necessary.

47. **Product Alternative Evaluation** — Compare user-provided product options by price, specifications, intended usage, durability information, and budget suitability.

48. **AI Price Target Tracking** — Store a desired buying price and identify when a recorded or externally verified price reaches that target.

49. **Purchase Goal Prioritization** — Rank goals by urgency, importance, deadline, and affordability while allowing the user to override AI recommendations.

50. **Multiple Goal Conflict Detection** — Identify when several purchase goals compete for the same money or deadlines and propose alternative contribution schedules.

51. **Goal Progress Prediction** — Forecast each goal's completion date from current savings, planned deposits, target cost, and available historical data.

52. **Budget Overrun Prevention** — Warn when the expected total cost, including user-entered delivery, tax, accessories, or additional fees, exceeds the planned amount.

53. **Postponement Impact Analysis** — Explain how delaying a purchase affects goal completion, available savings, and other planned expenses.

54. **Completed Purchase Summary** — After a goal is marked purchased, summarize the final cost, price difference from the original target, and remaining or surplus funds.

## 5. Calendar — AI Financial and Purchase Calendar

55. **AI Calendar Event Creation** — Convert natural-language instructions into calendar events, including purchase dates, savings deposits, subscription renewals, and payment deadlines.

56. **Smart Due-Date Extraction** — Extract dates from entered subscription details, receipts, invoices, and notes, then ask for confirmation when a date is unclear.

57. **Daily Financial Briefing** — Summarize the day's upcoming payments, planned purchases, savings contributions, and approaching financial deadlines.

58. **Weekly Purchase Outlook** — Summarize expected purchase costs, subscription charges, savings commitments, and the projected remaining budget for the coming week.

59. **Monthly Financial Forecast** — Predict scheduled outflows and planned purchases for the month using known commitments, recurring payments, and user-provided financial information.

60. **High-Spending Day Detection** — Highlight dates when several payments or purchase plans overlap and estimate their combined financial impact.

61. **Smart Reminder Timing** — Recommend reminder schedules based on due dates, purchase priority, and the user's chosen notification preferences.

62. **Calendar Conflict Detection** — Identify overlapping financial commitments, such as a large planned purchase scheduled near multiple subscription renewals.

63. **Flexible Deadline Rescheduling** — Suggest alternative dates for a purchase or savings contribution when the current plan conflicts with the user's budget.

64. **Recurring Event Prediction** — Generate expected future calendar entries for confirmed weekly, monthly, or yearly obligations and update them when schedules change.

65. **Past-Due Event Explanation** — Identify missed payments, purchase deadlines, or savings contributions and explain what action may be needed.

66. **Calendar-to-Budget Connection** — Calculate the estimated financial impact of upcoming calendar events and reflect confirmed changes in the user's planned budget.

67. **AI Event Summaries** — Explain what is financially important today, this week, or this month, including deadlines, high-cost dates, and potential conflicts.

## 6. Cross-Module AI Features

68. **Unified AI Financial Assistant** — Answer questions across purchases, subscriptions, savings, goals, and the calendar using the user's permitted app data.

69. **Natural-Language Financial Queries** — Answer requests such as “How much will I spend next month?” or “Can I afford my laptop goal after subscriptions?”

70. **Personalized Spending Forecast** — Estimate future spending using recorded purchases, recurring obligations, and confirmed upcoming plans, clearly identifying estimates.

71. **Available Money Calculation** — Estimate unallocated money after accounting for known commitments and user-defined savings reservations, while distinguishing planned funds from verified account balances.

72. **Smart Financial Notifications** — Prioritize alerts by urgency and financial impact, reducing repetitive or low-value notifications.

73. **AI Scenario Planning** — Compare scenarios such as buying now, waiting one month, reducing subscription costs, or increasing savings contributions.

74. **Financial Anomaly Detection** — Flag unusual purchase amounts, unexpected subscription changes, or duplicate-looking payments for the user to review rather than treating them as confirmed errors.

75. **Personalized AI Insights** — Generate concise, data-based observations about spending, saving consistency, recurring costs, and progress toward purchase goals.

76. **AI Monthly Review** — Summarize total recorded purchases, subscription costs, savings contributions, completed goals, missed targets, and notable changes during the month.

77. **Financial Data Quality Checks** — Detect missing amounts, inconsistent dates, incomplete recurring schedules, and suspicious duplicates that could make forecasts inaccurate.

78. **Explainable Recommendations** — Explain why a recommendation was made, which recorded information it used, and which assumptions could change the result.

79. **AI Memory and Preferences** — Remember user-approved preferences such as favorite categories, preferred vendors, savings habits, and typical reminder timing, with options to review or delete them.

80. **Privacy-Aware AI Processing** — Let users control which records the AI can analyze, minimize unnecessary data use, and clearly distinguish extracted facts from predictions or suggestions.

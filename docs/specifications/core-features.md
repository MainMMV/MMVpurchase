# MMV Purchases — Core Features (No AI)

## 1. MMV Purchases — Purchase Management

1. **Add Purchase** — Create a purchase with product name, price, quantity, vendor, category, purchase date, priority, and notes.
2. **Edit Purchase** — Update any purchase details whenever information changes.
3. **Delete Purchase** — Remove an unwanted purchase with a confirmation dialog.
4. **Purchase Categories** — Create, edit, delete, and assign custom categories with colors and icons.
5. **Vendor Management** — Save vendor names and reuse them when creating new purchases.
6. **Purchase Status** — Track purchases as Planned, Purchased, Postponed, or Cancelled.
7. **Purchase Priority** — Set Low, Medium, or High priority for every planned purchase.
8. **Quantity and Unit Price** — Calculate total cost automatically using unit price × quantity.
9. **Purchase Notes** — Add descriptions, product specifications, links, and personal notes.
10. **Receipt Attachments** — Attach receipt images or documents to a purchase.
11. **Search and Filters** — Search by product or vendor and filter by category, status, priority, price, or date.
12. **Sort Purchases** — Sort by newest, oldest, price, priority, name, and planned purchase date.
13. **Bulk Selection** — Select multiple purchases to delete, archive, change category, change status, or add tags.
14. **Purchase History** — Keep a history of completed and cancelled purchases.
15. **Purchase Statistics** — Display total planned purchases, completed purchases, category totals, and average purchase value.
16. **Tags and Labels** — Add custom tags such as Work, Home, Urgent, Gift, or Electronics.
17. **Archive Purchases** — Hide completed or old records without permanently deleting them.
18. **Data Export** — Export purchase records to CSV or Excel-compatible files.
19. **Duplicate Purchase** — Copy an existing purchase to quickly create a similar entry.
20. **Currency Settings** — Choose a preferred display currency and enter amounts in that currency. Automatic exchange rates are not required.

## 2. Subscription — Recurring Payment Management

21. **Add Subscription** — Save service name, price, billing frequency, start date, next payment date, category, and notes.
22. **Billing Frequency** — Support weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly, and custom recurring intervals.
23. **Automatic Next Due Date** — Calculate the next billing date after a payment is marked as paid.
24. **Upcoming Payment List** — Show subscriptions due today, this week, and later.
25. **Payment Reminders** — Schedule local notifications before a subscription payment is due.
26. **Subscription Status** — Manage Active, Paused, Cancelled, Expired, and Trial statuses.
27. **Payment History** — Record each payment with its date, amount, and payment status.
28. **Cost Summary** — Calculate estimated monthly and yearly subscription expenses.
29. **Trial Period Tracking** — Save trial start and end dates and show how many days remain.
30. **Price Change History** — Record subscription price changes and compare previous and current costs.
31. **Subscription Calendar** — Display payment due dates in a monthly calendar.
32. **Payment Method Details** — Store a payment method label, such as Cash, Card, or Bank Account. Avoid saving sensitive card credentials.
33. **Pause and Resume** — Pause a subscription and resume it with a new or adjusted billing schedule.
34. **Cancellation Tracking** — Record the cancellation date and keep the subscription in historical records.
35. **Overdue Payments** — Flag subscriptions whose due dates have passed without a recorded payment.

## 3. Savings — Savings Management

36. **Create Savings Fund** — Create separate funds such as Emergency Fund, Travel, Electronics, or Personal Savings.
37. **Set Savings Target** — Enter a target amount and an optional deadline for each savings fund.
38. **Add Savings Deposit** — Record money added to a savings fund.
39. **Withdraw Savings** — Record withdrawals with an amount, date, and optional reason.
40. **Savings Balance** — Calculate each fund's current balance from deposits and withdrawals.
41. **Progress Indicator** — Display the saved amount, remaining amount, and percentage of the target completed.
42. **Contribution Schedule** — Set a planned daily, weekly, or monthly contribution amount.
43. **Contribution Reminders** — Send local notifications on scheduled savings dates.
44. **Savings Transaction History** — View all deposits and withdrawals with dates and notes.
45. **Savings Milestones** — Display progress markers at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of the target.
46. **Deadline Calculator** — Calculate the approximate time needed to reach a target at the current contribution rate.
47. **Savings Categories** — Organize funds by purpose and assign custom colors or icons.
48. **Savings Reports** — Compare contributions and withdrawals across weeks, months, and years.
49. **Minimum Balance Alert** — Notify the user when a fund falls below a manually configured minimum.
50. **Completed Savings Goals** — Mark a fund as completed when the target is reached, while preserving its history.

## 4. Goal-Based Purchases — One-Time Purchases

51. **Create Purchase Goal** — Create a goal for a one-time purchase with a product name, target price, deadline, and priority.
52. **Set Target Budget** — Enter the maximum planned cost for the item.
53. **Goal Savings Balance** — Record how much money has already been saved toward the purchase.
54. **Remaining Amount Calculation** — Calculate target price minus the amount saved.
55. **Required Contribution Calculation** — Calculate the weekly or monthly contribution needed to reach the target by the deadline.
56. **Goal Progress Bar** — Display the saved amount and percentage completed.
57. **Goal Status** — Track goals as Planning, Saving, Ready to Buy, Purchased, Postponed, or Cancelled.
58. **Purchase Deadline Reminder** — Notify the user when the target date is approaching.
59. **Multiple Purchase Goals** — Manage several one-time purchase goals simultaneously.
60. **Goal Priority and Ranking** — Sort goals by priority, target date, price, or progress.
61. **Additional Cost Tracking** — Include delivery, taxes, accessories, installation, and other additional costs.
62. **Actual Purchase Price** — Enter the final amount paid and compare it with the original budget.
63. **Goal Completion Record** — Store the actual purchase date, final price, and any notes when the item is purchased.
64. **Postpone a Goal** — Change a target date without losing existing savings records.
65. **Goal-to-Savings Connection** — Link a savings fund to a purchase goal so its balance can contribute to the goal's progress without counting the same money twice.

## 5. Calendar — Purchase and Payment Calendar

66. **Monthly Calendar View** — Display planned purchases, subscription payments, savings contributions, and goal deadlines by date.
67. **Weekly and Daily Views** — View upcoming events by week or day.
68. **Create Calendar Event** — Add a custom event with a title, date, time, description, and reminder.
69. **Purchase Date Events** — Display planned purchase dates directly on the calendar.
70. **Subscription Due Dates** — Automatically show upcoming subscription payments based on saved billing schedules.
71. **Savings Contribution Dates** — Show scheduled contributions to savings funds.
72. **Goal Deadline Events** — Display target dates for one-time purchase goals.
73. **Local Notifications** — Send reminders using the device's notification system, subject to operating-system permissions.
74. **Recurring Calendar Events** — Repeat events daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.
75. **Event Editing** — Change the date, time, reminder, or details of a calendar event.
76. **Overdue Event Indicators** — Highlight events whose deadlines have passed.
77. **Daily Overview** — Show today's planned purchases, due payments, scheduled contributions, and deadlines.
78. **Upcoming Events List** — Display upcoming financial events in chronological order.
79. **Calendar Filters** — Show or hide purchases, subscriptions, savings, and goals independently.
80. **Event Completion** — Mark an event as completed while preserving its date and history.

## 6. General App Features

81. **Home Dashboard** — Display purchase totals, upcoming subscriptions, savings progress, active purchase goals, and today's events.
82. **Monthly Budget** — Set a monthly spending limit and compare recorded expenses against it.
83. **Planned vs. Actual Spending** — Compare planned purchase costs with actual completed purchase costs.
84. **Financial Summary** — Calculate recorded spending, subscription expenses, savings deposits, and withdrawals using saved data.
85. **Category Reports** — Show spending totals by category using basic charts and calculations.
86. **Date Range Reports** — View purchase and payment summaries for a selected period.
87. **Dark and Light Themes** — Support manual theme selection and optionally follow the device's system setting.
88. **Custom Appearance** — Customize accent colors, category colors, icons, and display preferences.
89. **Backup and Restore** — Export app data to a backup file and restore it later.
90. **Local Data Storage** — Store records in a local database so the basic app works without an internet connection.
91. **Optional Account Sync** — Add cloud synchronization only if a backend is introduced later; it is not required for the core version.
92. **App Lock** — Protect app access using device authentication or a supported local PIN mechanism.
93. **Settings and Preferences** — Configure currency display, week start day, reminders, theme, and default categories.
94. **Data Validation** — Prevent invalid prices, negative quantities where not allowed, missing required fields, and incorrect dates.
95. **Confirmation and Undo** — Confirm destructive operations and provide an undo option where practical.
96. **Empty States** — Show helpful instructions when a list, calendar, history, or report has no data.
97. **Transaction Audit Trail** — Preserve important record changes where needed, particularly for payment and savings history.
98. **App Data Search** — Search across purchases, subscriptions, savings funds, goals, vendors, and calendar events.
99. **Data Import** — Import supported CSV files to populate purchases or transaction history.
100. **Offline-First Operation** — Allow core features to work without AI, API keys, external services, or an internet connection.

## Recommended Core Structure

- **Home** — Dashboard and financial overview.
- **Purchases** — Planned purchases, completed purchases, categories, vendors, and history.
- **Subscriptions** — Recurring payments, trials, due dates, and payment history.
- **Savings** — Savings funds, deposits, withdrawals, and progress.
- **Goals** — One-time purchase goals and target tracking.
- **Calendar** — All scheduled purchases, payments, contributions, and deadlines.
- **Settings** — Currency, themes, reminders, backup, and data management.
